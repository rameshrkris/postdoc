About Me

Welcome! I am a postdoctoral researcher exploring General Relativity, Modified Gravity, and QFT in Curved spacetime. My work bridges the gap between Gravity and QFT in Curved Spacetime, utilizing analytical and numerical methods like Spectral theory, Exceptional Points, Continuous Fractions, Time-Domain Analysis, and WKB Analysis.

I completed my Ph.D. at Baylor University, under the supervision of Dr. Gerald Cleaver. You can download my complete Curriculum Vitae (CV) here.
